Preamble & Overview

Considering the encouragement and development of cooperative exchange in educational areas of mutual interest as desirable, each party wishes to expand the basis for friendship through educational exchanges between Japan and India. Therefore, Meishu Gakuen High School, Hitachi (MGHS) and Rishikul Vidyapeeth Haryana, India (RVP) set forth the understanding and desires in this Memorandum Of Understanding (hereafter MOU) with a view to continuing cooperation in educational exchanges.

1 Purpose of Exchange

  • Both parties agree to encourage their respective school bodies to cooperate in running joint school cross-cultural courses (exchange programs).
  • Both parties agree to encourage the establishment of sister-school links between MGHS and RVP with limited flexibility of students and staff participating (see Section 2.0 Exchanges).
  • Both parties are willing to further strengthen cooperation in education, and extend cooperation across all fields of education, when and as desirable.

2 Exchanges, Duration & Costs

Delegation Quotas: Both parties agree to provide opportunities for up to 6 students and up to 2 staff members to visit the sister school based on mutual arrangements.

Duration: To enhance mutual understanding and communication, each party will welcome one visit of approximately 10 days by delegations from the other party per annum throughout the period of the agreement.

Costs & Accommodations:

  • Visiting party pays costs of travelling and insurance for the duration of the visit.
  • Hosting party pays the cost of accommodation, sustenance, local transportation, and other local hospitalities.
  • Special (non-pre-planned) activities desired by the visiting party may be co-funded upon discussion.

3 Coordinators

In order to fulfill the goals of the MOU, coordinators for both MGHS and RVP are nominated to oversee development and management of joint exchange activities: David O' Donoghue (MGHS) and Archana Sharma (RVP).

4 Basis for Cooperation

  • Visiting parties will respect the cultural background of the hosting party when activities are proposed/planned.
  • Hosting party shall be cognisant of the visiting party's cultural background and work to accommodate student needs for meaningful international exchange.
  • Discussions will be conducted openly and frankly for mutually beneficial outcomes at all times.
  • Objectives lead to cooperative sister school relationships.

5 Activation & Signatories

This MOU comes into effect on the date of its signature (25th October 2023) and will be evaluated on an ongoing basis for necessary revisions or amendments.

Frequently Asked Questions for Future Teachers

Contextual guidance and operational tips when running exchanges under this MOU.

Can we increase the delegation size beyond 6 students if funding allows?

The MOU specifies "up to 6 students and up to 2 staff members". While flexibility is encouraged, any expansion beyond these limits requires formal consultation and mutual agreement between coordinators David O' Donoghue and Archana Sharma.

Who is responsible for student medical insurance during the exchange?

Per Section 2 (Costs and Accommodations), the visiting party is explicitly responsible for paying the costs of travelling and comprehensive insurance for the duration of the visit.

What cultural considerations are emphasized in the agreement?

Section 4 (Basis for Cooperation) mandates that visiting parties respect the cultural background of the hosting party, and hosting parties must be cognisant of visiting student needs.
